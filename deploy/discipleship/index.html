03 — The Way Of Jesus

With Him. Like Him. Sent By Him.

UP. IN. OUT.

At VF we are obsessed with Jesus. We want to be with Him, and we want to be like Him. In Scripture we see Jesus moving fluidly and purposefully through these three relationships—not as a checklist, but as a way of life that keeps looping.

01UPUP

Love God.

Relationship With God

Worship, prayer and obedience to God the Father. This is the vertical dimension of faith: being with Jesus and becoming like Him.

02ININ

Make Disciples.

Be A Disciple. Make Disciples.

Community, discipleship and fellowship with other believers. We build one another up and learn to follow Jesus together.

03OUTOUT

Impact The World.

Impact The World

Outreach to people far from God, service and justice in a broken world. We share the Gospel and help people in need.

UP → IN → OUT → UP. Keep following Jesus; keep inviting others into the journey.
