01 — The Invitation

Jesus Did Not Say “Come And Watch”

He Said,
“Follow Me.”

Discipleship is the lifelong process of becoming more like Jesus—and helping someone else do the same. It is not a class you finish. It is a life you practice with God and other people. We do this in three ways.

01Love GodUP

Love God

We Turn Our Attention Toward God And Learn To Live Aware Of His Presence.

  • Passionate Worship
  • Personal And Shared Prayer
  • Scripture And Spiritual Practices
  • An Expectant Life With God

How We Live It Out

  • We pray together daily on Zoom from 6:30–7:00am ↗
  • We worship on Sunday mornings together at 9:00am & 11:00am weekly (in person and online)
  • Daily Bible Reading Plan ↗
  • We fast as a church weekly from Sunday evenings after dinner until dinner on Mondays. See our social media for prayer points.
02Make DisciplesIN

Make Disciples

We Follow Jesus Together, Becoming Disciples Who Can Help Others Follow Him, Too.

  • Authentic Community
  • Growth Through Obedience
  • Serving One Another
  • Life Shared With Intention

How We Live It Out

  • Join a House Group →
  • Lead or start a Discovery Bible Study: get the Waha app ↗
  • Connect to a recovery ministry at VF: Celebrate Recovery, Men’s Healthy Sexuality, AA, GriefShare, Men of Courage, Women of Warfare ↗
  • Serve on a Sunday, in Kids or Student Ministries, on our First Impressions Team or Media/Worship Team: Sign up to volunteer →
03Impact The WorldOUT

Impact The World

We Carry The Good News And Practical Love Of Jesus Into The Places We Already Live.

  • Sharing The Gospel
  • Serving Our Community
  • Seeking People Far From God
  • Local And Global Mission

How We Live It Out

  • Serve at VF with one of our outreach ministries: Monthly Grocery Giveaways, Local Reset Ministries sober-living house, The Vinedresser Foster and Kinship ↗, other outreach events and more.
  • Go on a mission trip with us to Appalachia at Red Bird, or globally to Greece or Latvia ↗.
