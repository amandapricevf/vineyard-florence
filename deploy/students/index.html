01 — Vineyard Students

Love God. Make Disciples. Share Jesus.

Students Following
Jesus For A Lifetime.

At Vineyard Florence, students experience an exciting environment where they learn about Jesus and the Bible in a Spirit-led way. The heartbeat of Vineyard Students is to love God, make lifelong disciples of Jesus and share Jesus with the world around us. We are committed to partnering with parents and guardians as they disciple their students.
