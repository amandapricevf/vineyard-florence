01Our Team

Different Callings. One Shared Yes.

People Who Are
Here For People.

Ministry is never a one-person story. Meet the people helping Vineyard Florence make room for worship, belonging, healing and mission.

Lead Pastor

Ryan Snow

Ryan has led VF since 2021 and has served within the Vineyard movement since 1993. His heart is prayer, discipleship and multiplication.

Associate & Student Pastor

Amanda Price

As Associate and Student Pastor, Amanda helps people build a whole-life faith and become disciples who make disciples, drawing on rich ministry and music training.

Outreach & Recovery Pastor

Amber Brown

Amber serves people in difficult places through recovery ministry, community partnerships and practical care for families across Northern Kentucky.

Kids Ministry Director

Hanna McGuire-Jaeger

Hanna brings joy, energy and a deep commitment to helping children know Jesus while partnering closely with parents and caregivers.

Worship & Social Media Director

Myles Roberts

Myles creates spaces where people can connect with God, combining years of worship leadership with a love for music and storytelling.

Administrator & Vinedresser Founder

Karla Snow

Karla supports the church office and founded The Vinedresser, providing clothing and essentials to foster and kinship families.

Finance & Bookkeeping Director

Carol Cui

Carol stewards Vineyard Florence’s finances and loves growing in faith, following Jesus and serving the church alongside her family.

Custodial Care Leader

Denise Willen

Denise leads the team that cares for the church building and also serves through prayer, House Groups and The Vinedresser.

Maintenance Director

Chris Cousin

Chris is an ordained pastor, Maintenance Director and leader of the Men of Courage ministry, bringing a servant’s heart, practical skill and longstanding outreach experience.
