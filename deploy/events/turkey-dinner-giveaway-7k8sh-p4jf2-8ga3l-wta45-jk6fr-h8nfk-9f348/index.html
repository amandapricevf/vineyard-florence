Turkey Dinner Giveaway

Turkey Dinner
Giveaway.

Help bless families in need with a complete Thanksgiving meal. There are three ways to be part of it.

Sponsor a meal. A $25 gift provides a meal bag for a family. Text “$25 Thanksgiving” to 1-833-263-0025, or give online to the Thanksgiving fund.

Fill a bag. Pick up a bag and shopping list at the church doors and return it filled by Sunday, November 15.

Need a meal? Apply below and someone will reach out within two business days.
