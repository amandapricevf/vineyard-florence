07 — Good questions

Before You Arrive

Ask Away.

Clear answers for the things people actually wonder about before visiting—no church-speak decoder required.

Still Curious?

There Are No Silly Questions. Some Are Just Better Over Coffee.

Ask us something ↗
What should I wear?

Come as you are—jeans, a dress, a hoodie, work clothes, whatever feels like you. We mainly care that you are here. We do, however, recommend wearing clothes; beyond that, our fashion committee has been permanently disbanded.

When should I arrive?

Plan to arrive about 15 minutes before either the 9:00 or 11:00 service. That gives you time to grab a good cup of coffee, meet someone, check in kids and find a seat without making an action-movie entrance at the last second.

How long is each service—and what happens?

Each service usually lasts 75–80 minutes. We spend about 30–35 minutes in worship, share communion together and hear a practical, Bible-centered message for another 30–35 minutes. We close by making room for prayer and the Holy Spirit—and still respect your lunch plans.

What should I expect if I bring kids?

A member of our Kids team will help you register and check in your children, answer your questions and guide you to the right kids area. First-time check-in takes a few extra minutes—tiny humans, surprisingly, come with logistics.

Will I be singled out because I am new?

No. We will not ask you to stand, introduce yourself or wear a blinking ‘new person’ sign. You can participate as much or as little as you feel comfortable. Friendly hellos are likely; a surprise speech is not.

What is the worship like?

Our worship is passionate, contemporary and focused on making room for God’s presence. You can sing, stand, listen or simply take it in. There is no vocal audition, and no one is grading your rhythm.

Am I expected to give money?

No. Giving is a step for people who call Vineyard Florence home, not a cover charge for walking through the door. If you are visiting, be our guest. The coffee is free and does not arrive with an invoice.

Can I come if I am not sure what I believe?

Absolutely. Questions, doubts and honest curiosity are welcome here. You do not need to clean up your life or finish your theology homework before you visit. None of us has everything figured out—and anyone who says otherwise probably needs more coffee.

Can someone pray with me?

Yes. We make room near the end of each service for prayer requests and personal prayer, and you can also connect with someone afterward. Share as much or as little as you want; God already knows the unabridged edition.
