01 — Vineyard Kids

Kids Ministry At Vineyard Florence

Know God.
Hear God. Share His Love.

At Vineyard Florence, our kids experience safe, exciting environments where they learn about Jesus in creative, Bible-based and Spirit-led ways. The heartbeat of Vineyard Kids is to show children who God is, teach them how they can hear God for themselves and provide fun ways to share His amazing love. It is our greatest honor to partner with parents and guardians in discipling their children. We would love to see your family this Sunday!

Planning which service to attend? Nursery, Toddlers and Preschool are available at the 11 AM service only. Elementary is available at both 9 AM and 11 AM.

03 — How Check-In Works

Simple. Secure.
Ready For Sunday.

01

Before Service

Check your child in at any kiosk in the Kids Cave in the lobby. You and your child will receive matching, visit-specific name tags. Your child wears one; you keep the matching tag.

02

During Service

If we need to contact you, your child's identification code will appear on the screens and we may send you a text. Exit the auditorium and a team member will direct you to the classroom.

03

After Service

Go to your child's classroom and show your matching tag before your child is released. If you would like prayer, please pick up your child first or send someone you trust with your tag.
