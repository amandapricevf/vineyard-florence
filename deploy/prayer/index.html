The Five-Step Prayer Model

Learn To Pray
For Someone.

This guide comes from the model shared by Vineyard Florence. God is the One who heals and leads. We listen, ask permission and care for the person in front of us.

  1. 01
    The Interview+

    Introduce yourself, then ask, “How can I pray for you?” or “Where does it hurt?” Listen carefully as you learn what the person needs.

  2. 02
    The Diagnosis+

    Ask why this person may have this condition. Listen for the underlying physical, emotional, relational or spiritual issue God may be inviting you to pray for.

  3. 03
    Prayer Selection+

    Ask what kind of prayer may help. You can also ask, “Lord, what do You want to heal right now?” Consider intercession, command, pronouncement, agreement or an appropriate prayer against spiritual oppression.

  4. 04
    Prayer Engagement+

    With permission, begin to pray and lay hands on the person respectfully when appropriate. Check in, ask further questions and keep listening to the Holy Spirit.

  5. 05
    Post-Prayer Direction+

    Offer a simple next step whether healing occurred or not. Encourage continued prayer, Scripture, fellowship and a deep relationship with God. When healing occurs, encourage medical confirmation and document what God did.

Prayer supports compassionate care and does not replace appropriate medical or mental-health treatment.
