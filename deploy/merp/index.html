01 — The Partnership

A New Beginning Can Carry Hope

So Much Left Behind.
So Much Still Ahead.

Across the Mediterranean, millions of men, women and families have fled the Middle East in search of safety and religious freedom. Many have left behind homes, careers, loved ones and everything else familiar. In the middle of that painful upheaval, many are also searching for hope, freedom and a true connection to God.

Founded and led by Vineyard Florence Church, in partnership with several other Vineyard churches, the Middle Eastern Refugee Partnership exists to see everyday disciple-making, church-planting movements and humanitarian relief take root among Middle Eastern refugees and asylum seekers from nations where religious freedom is restricted.

Our Vision

Know Jesus.
Be Transformed.
Be Released To Lead.

We long to see people come to know Jesus, be transformed by His love and be released as leaders who make disciples who make disciples. We believe people who have been displaced are not only those we serve, but those God is raising up to lead healthy next-generation churches, families and communities.

02 — How It Works

Start Together.
Multiply Locally.

Support from partner churches helps the work begin. Investment in local leaders helps it grow and continue.

01

We Start Together.

New church plants are launched with the support of partner churches in the United States, who provide initial oversight, training when needed, prayer and funding.

02

We Build Local Leaders.

From the beginning, we invest in raising up leaders from within refugee communities themselves, equipping them to disciple and train others and shepherd new believers.

03

We Hand It Off.

Over time, leadership and ownership of each church plant transition to local churches in Turkey, Greece, North Macedonia, Central Europe, Cyprus and beyond, so the work can continue and multiply long after we step back.

Practical Care. A Viable Future.

A Place To Live.
A Way To Thrive.

We Create Self-Sustaining Businesses And Residences For Women.

The best track for residency and a viable contribution to a new country is to have legal addresses and fair-wage jobs. MERP works to create self-sustaining businesses and residences that help women build that future.
