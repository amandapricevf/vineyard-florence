01 — When We Baptize

First Sunday
Of Each Month.

We have baptisms on the first Sunday of each month, during our second service at 11:00 AM. A baptism class begins at 10:30 AM, before that service.

Approach any of our staff with your interest to be baptized, and we will let you know when the next baptism is scheduled.

02 — What It Means

A New Life,
Celebrated.

At Vineyard Florence, baptism is going public about your faith in Jesus and communicating to the world your heartfelt commitment to following Him. Being baptized shows others that you have decided to follow Jesus and allows them to celebrate this decision with you.

Baptism illustrates Christ’s burial and resurrection and your new life as a Christian. Baptism doesn’t make you a believer—it shows that you already believe. More importantly, baptism does not “save” you—only your faith in Christ does that.

Baptizing Kids

We love getting to baptize kids, but we want to make sure they have talked about it with their parents and understand what they are doing and what it means to say Jesus is the Lord of their lives.

Baptized When You Were Younger?

If you were baptized when you were younger but have personally decided to follow God yourself, you are welcome to be baptized again to display your new commitment.

Have Questions?

We would love to help answer them for you. Send us a message and we’ll connect with you soon.
