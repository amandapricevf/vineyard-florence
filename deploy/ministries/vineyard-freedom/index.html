01 — About

Freedom, Identity
And Wholeness.

Vineyard Freedom is designed to help you experience the freedom, healing and restoration that Jesus offers us. Through prayer, biblical truth and the leading of the Holy Spirit, we walk alongside those seeking breakthrough from spiritual oppression, generational cycles, emotional wounds, trauma and strongholds.

Completing the intake questionnaire helps our ministry team prayerfully prepare to serve you with care, wisdom, humility and confidentiality.

This process is not about condemnation, but about freedom, identity and wholeness in Christ. If you are ready to pursue deeper healing and walk in freedom, we are honored to walk this journey with you.

02 — The Process

Your Next
Step.

  1. Fill out the intake questionnaire if you want to take the next step in experiencing Jesus’ healing, freedom and deliverance.
  2. Once we receive your questionnaire, we will assign a ministry team to you, and they will reach out with next steps.
  3. The ministry team will read, fast and pray through your questionnaire and make notes on anything the Holy Spirit highlights to them. Then they will reach out to you to schedule a prayer session.
  4. The ministry team will follow up with you within a week of your prayer session, with any resources to help you continue to walk in freedom: referrals to counseling, daily declarations, prayer, how to renew your mind, addiction resources, marriage resources, book recommendations and more.

If you are ready to experience the freedom Jesus has for you, start with the intake questionnaire.
